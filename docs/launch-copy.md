## Short Description

Create a room, invite your people, vote yes/maybe/no, and let Plan Roulette turn the group mood into a final plan with an itinerary.

## Long Description

Plan Roulette is for the moment when everyone says "I'm down for anything" and somehow nothing happens.

Start with a few constraints: budget, time, energy, weather, group size, and the kind of thing you are in the mood for. Plan Roulette builds a local deck of plan ideas, then gives your group a simple vote: yes, maybe, or no.

No essays. No guilt. No one has to pretend they want a huge night out when they want coffee, snacks, or a walk with a clear exit plan.

When the votes are in, Plan Roulette picks the best fit, explains why it won, and turns it into a shareable itinerary with steps, budget, timing, backup plan, and who is in or maybe.

Use it for dinner, coffee, study breaks, rainy-day plans, game nights, walks, low-key events, date ideas, and "please just choose something" nights.

## Review Prompt Timing

Ask for a review after a successful final-plan share or after a user saves a completed room, not during voting or while someone is trying to join.

Good moments:

- After the user shares a final itinerary.
- After the user saves their second completed room.
- After the user returns to a saved plan from history.

Avoid:

- Before a group has reached a result.
- After a no-consensus result.
- After an error, offline state, or permission denial.
- During guest-to-account upgrade.

## Privacy Positioning

Plan Roulette only needs the details required to make group planning work: account/session state, room membership, votes, generated plan options, saved rooms, and minimal product analytics.

We do not need private group-chat history, contacts, payment data, or location tracking for the MVP. Invites are link-based, analytics avoid sensitive free text, and client Supabase access uses public anon keys backed by strict Row Level Security.
